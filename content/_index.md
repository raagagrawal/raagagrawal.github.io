**Raag Agrawal, Ph.D.**

MD Candidate, Class of 2027 · [UCLA-Caltech Medical Scientist Training Program](https://mstp.healthsciences.ucla.edu/)

I am a physician-scientist in training at the UCLA-Caltech Medical Scientist Training Program, now in my clinical years and pursuing a career in **radiation oncology**. I completed my PhD in Human Genetics in 2025 in the [Boutros Lab](https://www.uclahealth.org/departments/urology/iuo/research/faculty-labs/dr-paul-boutros-lab), where I used whole-genome sequencing and integrative genomics to define molecular subtypes of high-risk prostate cancer and the biology behind PSMA PET imaging.

My interests sit at the intersection of **radiation oncology**, **genitourinary oncology**, and **cancer genomics**: using molecular and computational tools to decide which patients benefit from which treatments — including for the patients our current tools serve least well.

Before UCLA, I graduated *Cum Laude* from Columbia University as an I.I. Rabi Scholar, and spent a year at the University of Cambridge and the Wellcome Sanger Institute working on the Human Cell Atlas.

I'm also open to select **[consulting and VC scout work](/consulting/)** in oncology, genomics, and computational biology.
