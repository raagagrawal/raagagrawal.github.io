## Where I can help

**Scientific & technical due diligence.** Evaluating the biology, data, and clinical claims behind oncology, precision-medicine, and diagnostics companies — including genomic biomarkers, molecular imaging (PSMA PET), and the evidence needed to move a product into the clinic.

**Computational genomics & bioinformatics.** Whole-genome sequencing and variant calling, methylation, transcriptomics, and single-cell RNA-seq; building reproducible Nextflow pipelines; reviewing analysis plans and results for rigor.

**Oncology real-world data & trials.** Translational correlatives for clinical trials, multi-omic analysis of trial cohorts, and the realities of turning real-world oncology data into usable evidence.

**VC scouting.** Sourcing and first-pass screening of early-stage companies in cancer therapeutics, diagnostics, radiopharmaceuticals, and computational biology, with a clinician's perspective on unmet need and adoption.
